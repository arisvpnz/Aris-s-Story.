MY STORY
Introduction

Hello. My name is Aris.

Also known as Silver. Dav. Gay boy. And more and more names that were never mine to carry.

I was born on 8th December, 2011. That makes me 14 years old.

Sadly, I have had a lung infection since birth. On top of that, I was diagnosed with ADHD. In short — ADHD means I cannot stay still, I get distracted easily, and my mind never stops. I will explain my whole story. So please — stay.

Primary School — Age 6

At the age of 6, it was my first day going to primary school in the Netherlands. I was pretty lonely at first — but I quickly found a best friend. We'll call him Job2.

Me and Job2 grew up together. We were close. Real close.

But in 6th–8th class — the last years before high school — my life became HELL.

I was being bullied. Constantly. For my ADHD. For my tics — tics are when you repeatedly do something over and over, uncontrollably, usually triggered by stress.

My teachers were racist towards me. All except one.

That one teacher had the same problems as me. ADHD. He was a gay man — which I fully supported, even as a Muslim. He told me to ignore the bad teachers. He would protect me. He promised.

When the time came for grades — a corrupt teacher tried to give me VMBO (the lowest level) to push me down, even though my score was high enough for HAVO.

That man stepped in immediately. Him and my parents had a fierce, harsh discussion with the school. They threatened to involve the police over the racism. The corrupt teacher got scared and corrected the grade.

I went to the school I had scored for.

I thanked that man with everything I had. His name will always live in my mind.

High School — A New Beginning

Me and Job2 didn't end up in the same class, but we were in the same school. I was alone for a while.

Then I met someone. We'll call him Job1 — my ex best friend. He asked if I wanted to be friends. I said yes. Of course I did.

Things were good. I made it to second class. Things seemed okay.

Until my mother stepped in.

My mother has always pushed me with harsh motivation. Bad grades. Trouble. She called me gay — for my voice, for how I dressed, for how skinny I am, for how I don't eat enough. She blamed my lack of focus on games — even though I studied 6 hours a day and barely ever failed.

It was never enough. It never will be.

The Betrayal

I found out that Job1's parents were racist towards me — because I am Muslim and Turkish. They called me names. Ugly names.

Then, through my coding skills, I found a message in a data breach. Someone had partnered with Job1 to leak my address, school, name, and my old crush to strangers. Someone close to the situation told me the truth. I am grateful for that.

I confronted Job1. He didn't care. He made things worse. Ignored me. His friends accused me of bullying him — when it was always the other way around.

His parents told him to cut all contact with me forever. And he listened. Just like that.

Everything at Once

Things kept crumbling.

I lost my girlfriend of 9 months — she said I was too boring. My mom kept stressing me. I had no friends. Not online. Not in real life. Just nothing.

The pressure from all sides — school, home, loneliness — broke something in me.

On 3rd September, I tried to end it all. I got scared and stopped. But it didn't get better.

A few days later, things got even worse. The internet turned on me too.

On 18th December, I cut myself. Deeply. And I tried to hang myself.

But before it was the end — I stopped. I picked up the phone and dialed 113 — the Dutch suicide prevention line.

I explained everything. They gave me what I needed. So I listened.

I tried to become myself again. I stood up to my mother. Being firm. And slowly — slowly — it started to work.

Online — New People

Then the internet started to change. Internet bullying pushed things down again. But I had made some friends online.

Adel. Angelo. Freya.

All three of them were genuinely kind. My heart made its choices quickly — Angelo and Freya became very good friends. And Adel... I found myself falling for her.

I talked to Adel a lot. Complimented her. Was happy. Really happy.

But she told me she already cared about someone. Someone we'll call Rob8. And Rob8 had told her that he only wanted lust — not love.

I told her it wasn't worth it. She got a little upset with me. I apologised. I stayed sweet. I felt bad for getting too deep into her personal things.

And that's when it all started to pile up again. That's when I reached the edge.

26 March, 2026.

I was about to end it.

I had started a recording. In my left hand — a rusty knife.

In the video, I explained everything. Who caused this. What happened. And I thanked everyone — for the pain they gave me that brought me to this moment.

Then I updated my status. My server of 200 members saw it. And the most important people —

Adel. Angelo. Freya.

They all started mass DMing me. At the same time. All of them. Pleading.

“Don't do it. Please. We're here.”

I was crying.

Did people really care about me? Or was this all a joke?

I explained everything to them — as short as I could put it:

This may sound childish. But —

I don't have IRL friends.
My mother doesn't love me.
My mother calls me gay.
I get bullied at school.
I get bullied for the hair on my body — I shaved it because of the pressure.
My girlfriend left me.
I get bullied for my looks, my ADHD, my tics, my clothing style.
My best friend betrayed me.
I get bullied for calling a suicide prevention number.
My family bullies me too.

It's all hard.

They comforted me. It helped — a little.

But I still couldn't stop.

Until Adel told me — she cares about me. That the other person doesn't matter. That she really cares.

I got emotional. I asked her through tears:

“...Are you really telling the truth?”

She said things to me. Things I needed to hear.

I gave her a choice — to let me do it, or to stop me.

She told me, with everything she had: Don't do it. Please.

I listened.

I dropped the knife.

27 MARCH.
Things are going a little better.
For now.
